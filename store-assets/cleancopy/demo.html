How to Brew Better Pour-Over Coffee

Pour-over rewards patience and consistency. A few small changes make a bigger difference than expensive gear.

What you need

  • 20 g of freshly ground coffee
  • 320 g of water at 94°C
  • A paper filter and a gooseneck kettle

The method

Rinse the filter, then bloom the grounds with 40 g of water for 30 seconds. Pour the rest in slow circles and aim to finish in about three minutes. See the grind size guide if your brew tastes bitter.
