# CleanCopy Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

CleanCopy is a browser extension that copies the readable content of the current web page to your clipboard as Markdown.

## What the extension accesses

When you click the CleanCopy toolbar icon, the extension reads the content of the page in the active tab (its text, links, images' addresses and structure) in order to convert it to Markdown. It does this only at the moment you click the icon, and only for that tab.

## What happens to that content

- The conversion runs entirely inside your browser.
- The resulting Markdown is written to your clipboard and nothing else is done with it.
- Page content is **not** sent to any server, **not** stored by the extension, and **not** shared with anyone.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising identifiers.
- It does not make network requests.
- It does not load or run remote code.
- It does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

## Permissions

- `activeTab` and `scripting`: read the current page when you click the icon.
- `offscreen` and `clipboardWrite`: write the generated Markdown to your clipboard.
