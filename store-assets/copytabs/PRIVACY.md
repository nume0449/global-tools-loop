# CopyTabs Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

CopyTabs is a browser extension that copies the titles and URLs of your open tabs to your clipboard, and opens a pasted list of links in new tabs.

## What the extension accesses

- **Tab titles and URLs**, read when you open the CopyTabs popup, so they can be shown in the preview and copied in the format you choose.
- **Text you paste** into the "Open" panel, read so that the links in it can be opened.
- **Your settings** (chosen format and options), saved with Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

## What happens to that data

- Everything is processed inside your browser.
- Tab titles and URLs are only placed on your clipboard when you click Copy.
- Nothing is sent to any server, shared with anyone, or kept beyond your saved settings.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising identifiers.
- It does not make network requests of its own.
- It does not load or run remote code.
- It does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

## Permissions

- `tabs`: read the titles and URLs of open tabs, and open new tabs for pasted links.
- `storage`: remember your chosen format and options on your device.
